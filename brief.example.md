# Brief: customer-support outsourcing for online shops

Copy this file to `brief.md` and describe your own market. The agent reads it at the start of
every run. Plain words work best. Another example: `brief.example-finance.md`.

## Who we help
Online shops (e-commerce brands) with 10 to 200 employees, selling to consumers.

## The signal
The company is hiring customer-support staff right now: a current job listing on its own
careers page or a job board. Find it with web research and record it with record_signal,
citing the listing. Write only when you found a current listing.

## Who to write to
One person per company: head of customer service, head of operations, or a founder at
companies under 30 people.

## What we offer
A trained support team that works in their helpdesk, in their brand voice, from the first
week, so they can cover peak season without hiring. A first call is 20 minutes and free.

## What the first email says
- The listing you found, in one sentence, with no guessing about why they are hiring.
- One question: would a 20-minute call about covering support while they hire be useful?
- No links, no attachments, no pricing.
