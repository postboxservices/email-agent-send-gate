# Brief: outsourced finance for companies that just raised funding

An example brief for a finance service. Copy it to `brief.md` and make it yours.

## Who we help
Private companies with 20 to 300 employees that announced a funding round in the last 90 days.

## The signal
A public announcement of the round (the company's own news page, a press release or a news
article) dated within 90 days. Find it with web research and record it with record_signal,
citing the page. Write only when you found it.

## Who to write to
One person per company: the CFO, head of finance, or a founder at companies under 50 people.

## What we offer
Month-end close, investor reporting and cash-flow forecasting handled by a dedicated team, so
the new money is tracked properly from the first board meeting. A first call is 20 minutes and
free.

## What the first email says
- The announcement you found, in one sentence, with the date.
- One question: would a 20-minute call about investor reporting after the round be useful?
- No links, no attachments, no pricing, no congratulations padding.
